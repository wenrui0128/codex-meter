CodexMeter - unsigned local build

Install:
1. Drag CodexMeter to the Applications folder.
2. On first launch, if macOS says the developer cannot be verified:
   - Right-click CodexMeter in Finder and choose Open;
   - or allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Requirements:
- Codex must already be signed in on this Mac, with ~/.codex/auth.json present.
- The app reads tokens.access_token from ~/.codex/auth.json to request ChatGPT quota endpoints.
- The app does not show access tokens, refresh tokens, cookies, or complete unique IDs in the UI.

Quit:
- Right-click the menu bar icon and choose Quit.
